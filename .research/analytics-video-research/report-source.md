# Research source ledger — analytics / ML analytics interview preparation

Research date: 2026-08-26 (Europe/Moscow)

Scope: Russian- and English-language lectures, conference recordings and realistic case discussions for a data/product analyst who will discuss metrics, experiments, search/recommendation quality, ML evaluation and ML System Design. YouTube was prioritized; official Yandex pages were used to verify descriptions and dates where YouTube pages were throttled.

## Source quality scale

- **P1** — primary source: Yandex/company channel, speaker’s own conference recording, or official product/education page.
- **P2** — strong expert or conference recording from a recognized community/company.
- **P3** — useful secondary explanation or interview simulation; use after the primary sources.

## Research limitations

- YouTube pages were sometimes throttled; metadata was cross-checked through YouTube search results and official Yandex Education pages when available.
- No claim here says that a video is guaranteed to appear in a particular interview. The ranking reflects topical fit for the stated preparation goal.
- View counts and “recently published” labels are not used as quality evidence. Duplicated uploads were collapsed to one canonical link where possible.
